How it works

Three steps.
Then it runs itself.

No spreadsheets. No manual logging. Your inbox is your source of truth.

Step 01
Install the extension
One click from the Chrome Web Store. No account, no signup, no credit card. Just install and open.
Step 02
Connect Gmail
Grant read-only Gmail access. We scan the last 6 months of your inbox for job-related emails — one time.
Step 03
See your pipeline
Every application instantly organized. Applied, Screening, Interview, Offer, Rejected — all in one matrix view. Auto-syncs every 30 minutes.
Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Built for job seekers who are serious about their search, not their spreadsheets.

Job matrix view
See every company and role as a row. Stages as columns. Instantly see where each application stands.
Data stays in your browser
No servers. No database. All job data is stored locally in Chrome. Only you can see it.
TAT tracking
See how many days it took to go from Applied to Screening, Interview, or Offer for every company.
Auto-sync
Scans Gmail every 30 minutes in the background. New recruiter emails classified automatically.
Job deduplication
Multiple emails from the same company are merged into one row. See the full journey in one click.
NLP classification
Weighted keyword scoring identifies Applied, Screening, Interview, Offer, and Rejected from email content.
